PayPal Income Calculator

Add up what you were paid through PayPal, for each tax year or for any dates you choose.

You get:

  • your earnings, in the currency you were paid in
  • the same earnings in your own currency, at PayPal's rate (and at HMRC's, for pounds)
  • what you withdrew to your card or bank, to check against your statement

Your files are read on this device only. Nothing is uploaded, sent or saved anywhere.

A calculator, not tax advice. Your files never leave this device. Not connected to PayPal or HMRC.
